# Clausewise walkthrough

Use this as the narration and click plan for a 70-90 second submission video. It takes 12 clicks, stays focused on the running prototype, and avoids a feature-list presentation.

1. Open the deployed app and say: "This is Clausewise, a legal-information assistant that helps people understand what they are about to agree to."
2. Click **Employment offer demo**. Point out the clear dashboard: reading time, key obligations, and attention items.
3. Click **Notice & exit**. Say: "The review changes to the concern that matters to this person, rather than giving one generic summary."
4. Click the **Clause 4** source link. Open the clause and briefly show the original source text alongside the plain-language explanation.
5. Return to **Overview** and briefly show the source-linked review checklist, including the acceptance deadline and clarification tasks.
6. Click **Ask** and ask: "What happens if I resign?" Show the answer, its evidence excerpt, and the Clause 4 link.
7. Ask: "Does this include health insurance?" Show the explicit "not found in this document" response. Say: "It does not fill gaps with invented answers."
8. Click **Compare**, paste the provided short revised document, and click **Compare documents**. Show the changed notice period.
9. Return to **Overview**, point to the questions for a legal professional, and end with: "Clausewise gives people grounded information and better questions, not legal advice."

Before recording, deploy to Firebase Hosting, use 1440×900 or 1280×720 resolution, keep browser chrome out of frame, and use the actual demo interactions. Do not narrate implementation details or read the README on screen.
